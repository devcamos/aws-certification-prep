# AIF-C01 study plan

Exam date: **Fri 13 Nov 2026** (online, OnVUE). See [how-to-book](../how-to-book/).

## Approach

- Learn capability → do a lab → answer questions → log mistakes
- Spend more time on Domains 2 and 3 (they carry more weight)
- Leave the last stretch for official AWS practice and fresh timed mocks

## Suggested order

1. Domain 1 — AI/ML fundamentals (hierarchy, training vs inference, metrics)
2. Domain 2 — Generative AI (tokens, embeddings, transformers, hallucinations)
3. Domain 3 — Foundation model apps (prompts, RAG, fine-tuning, evaluation)
4. Domain 4 — Responsible AI (bias, fairness, human review, guardrails)
5. Domain 5 — Security and governance (IAM, encryption, TLS/mTLS, audit)

## Readiness targets (from my study checklist)

- Official exam guide reviewed for every task statement
- Official Practice Question Set completed
- AWS Official Practice Exam passed under timed conditions
- Three fresh timed mocks at about 85% or above
- No domain below about 80%
- Every wrong answer explained in [weak-spots.md](weak-spots.md)
