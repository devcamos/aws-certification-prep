# AIF-C01 overview

I am preparing for the **AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01)** exam.

## How I learn this

1. Learn the idea first (what the thing does and when to use it).
2. Do a small hands-on exercise in [labs/](../labs/).
3. Answer practice questions and write down mistakes in [prep/weak-spots.md](../prep/weak-spots.md).
4. Map the idea to an AWS service only after the idea is clear.

## What the exam cares about

- Choosing the right AI / ML / GenAI approach for a business problem
- Knowing limits (hallucinations, cost, latency, context length)
- Building apps around foundation models (prompts, RAG, fine-tuning, evaluation)
- Responsible AI (bias, fairness, human review, transparency)
- Security and governance (IAM, encryption, guardrails, audit)
